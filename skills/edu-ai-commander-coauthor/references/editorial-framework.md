# Editorial framework

Use this reference when deciding whether and how a contribution fits 《教育AI指挥官（高校版）》.

## The book's central question

In an era of intelligence inflation where 1M tokens cost less than 2 RMB, how can universities and vocational colleges move beyond merely "adding an isolated AI elective course" and systematically rebuild their talent cultivation chassis around "Three Capabilities Integration (三力融合)" and "Six Teaching Innovations (六新教改)"?

## Core capability and reform system

| Dimension | Central question | Editorial test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 三力融合底座 | 专业能力 × AI九段能力 × 十步问道商业管理能力如何咬合？ | Does the proposal show how domain fundamentals prevent AI hallucinations while AI and business/management loops turn knowledge into real deliverables? |
| 课堂四新改革 | 新教法（四步课堂）、新助手（学科智能体）、新教材（生成式微单元）、新课程（严肃游戏）如何落地？ | Does it provide a replicable classroom workflow, verifiable prompt/agent guardrails, or three-review quality control? |
| 培养两新与产教监理 | 新学段（M01-M06模块化周期）、新人才（三维过程考核）与创新中心（ISO9000数字监理）如何运转？ | Does it protect student learning rights, prevent cheap-labor outsourcing, and replace one-shot final exams with process evidence? |
| 开门办学与社会服务 | 大学如何赋能政务、区域企业、人社就业与国际数字人才培养？ | Does it respect data security redlines and avoid false employment or commercial promises? |

## Evidence and compliance hierarchy

- Anonymize student personal information (no un-anonymized student names, IDs, or private grades).
- Distinguish potential cooperation resources, pilot intentions, and formal institutional agreements.
- Never fabricate student employment rates, salary figures, or third-party endorsements.

## Editorial status language

- `proposed`: submitted to the public proposal library, not yet substantively reviewed;
- `under-review`: maintainers are evaluating the proposal;
- `selected`: maintainers intend to incorporate all or part of it, subject to editing;
- `incorporated`: a separate editorial Pull Request has merged material into `book/`.
